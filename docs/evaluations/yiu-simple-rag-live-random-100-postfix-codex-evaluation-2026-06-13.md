## Key Findings

1. **Basic retrieval is back.** Program existence, campus-program mapping, SHMYO/MYO lists, several fees, DGS/yatay geçiş, and identity questions are now answered directly.
2. **Fee retrieval is still inconsistent.** Some program fees are correct, but Tıp Fakültesi ücreti, tıp ücret, and İngilizce Tıp ücreti still fail or select the wrong international/YÖS audience.
3. **Wrong organization contamination remains severe.** Three answers referenced another university (Yıldırım Beyazıt Üniversitesi or Yıldız Technical University). This is a hard demo blocker because it destroys trust immediately.
4. **No-info is much lower but still often misses available facts.** Exact no-info dropped to 21%, but several misses are on known table/policy facts such as İlk ve Acil Yardım kontenjanı, hazırlık failure, Tıp clinical timing, kadavra, and microscope facts.
5. **Clarification behavior improved but is not perfect.** Identity and concrete institution context are better; remaining bad clarifications include cadaver, future hospital location, diploma abroad, and “Anestezi mi İlk ve Acil Yardım mı?” advisory prompts.
6. **Source selection still over-infers.** The bot sometimes answers from adjacent documents: campus safety from İSG, application-area from program existence, health clubs from a sports-club protocol, or housing from an unrelated psychologist contact.
